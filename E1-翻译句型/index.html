    翻译与句型 · Cross Reading Card¶

    上海卷结构：满分150分 | 翻译15分（5句×3分）+ 概要写作10分 + 引导写作25分

    面①：翻译题五步法¶

    步骤1：划提示词 → 找到题目要求的关键词（必须用）
步骤2：判时态语态 → 根据时间标志词确定时态和语态
步骤3：定主干结构 → 确定主谓宾/主系表
步骤4：补修饰成分 → 定语、状语、非谓语
步骤5：检查细节 → 单复数、冠词、介词搭配、拼写

    核心原则¶

    原则 说明
    提示词必用 题目给的关键词/短语必须出现在答案中
    语法准确 > 用词华丽 宁可简单句，不要复杂但错误的句子
    中式表达是扣分主因 避免逐字翻译，用英语思维组织
    时态一致性 同一句中多个动词时态要一致

    面②：翻译必考句型（按语法分类）¶

    1. 定语从句（每年必考1题）¶

    中文 英文句型 关键词
    ……的那个地方 the place where ... where
    ……的那个人 the person who/that ... who/that
    ……的那件事 the thing that/which ... which/that
    ……的原因 the reason why ... why
    正如我们所知 as is known to all as
    ……，这使得…… ..., which makes it possible to ... which

    例题：这是我第一次参观的那个博物馆，给我留下了深刻的印象。

    This is the museum which/that I visited for the first time, which left a deep impression on me.

    2. 状语从句（每年必考1题）¶

    类型 连接词 例句结构
    时间 when / while / as soon as / until He was reading when the phone rang.
    原因 because / since / as / now that Now that you have finished the work, you can go home.
    条件 if / unless / as long as / provided that You won't succeed unless you try your best.
    让步 although / though / even though / while Although he is young, he knows a lot.
    目的 so that / in order that He got up early so that he could catch the first bus.
    结果 so ... that / such ... that It was so cold that the water froze.

    3. 非谓语动词（高频）¶

    类型 用法 例句
    不定式 to do 目的、将来 He came here to study English.
    现在分词 doing 主动、进行 Seeing the teacher, he stood up.
    过去分词 done 被动、完成 The book written by him is very popular.
    独立主格 逻辑主语不同 Weather permitting, we will go out.

    4. 名词性从句¶

    类型 引导词 例句
    主语从句 That / What / Whether What he said surprised everyone.
    宾语从句 that / what / whether / if / wh- I wonder whether/if he will come.
    表语从句 that / what / whether The fact is that we don't have enough time.
    同位语从句 that The news that he won the prize is true.

    5. 强调句型 & 倒装句¶

    类型 结构 例句
    强调句 It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其他 It was in the park that I met him.
    not only...but also 倒装 Not only + 助动词 + 主语 + ... Not only did he study hard, but he also helped others.
    so/such...that 倒装 So + adj./adv. + 助动词 + 主语 + that So difficult was the exam that few passed it.
    only 倒装 Only + 状语 + 助动词 + 主语 Only then did I realize my mistake.
    never/seldom 倒装 否定副词 + 助动词 + 主语 Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.

    学习与成长¶

    中文 英文
    对……产生兴趣 develop/become an interest in
    充分利用 make full/good use of
    在……方面取得进步 make progress in
    养成……的习惯 form/develop the habit of
    集中精力于 concentrate/focus one's attention on
    对……有好处/坏处 be beneficial/harmful to
    为……做准备 prepare for / make preparations for
    尽最大努力 do/try one's best to
    与……相比 compared with/to
    在……的帮助下 with the help/assistance of

    社会与科技¶

    中文 英文
    随着科技的发展 with the development of technology
    在现代社会 in modern society
    在……方面起重要作用 play an important/part/key role in
    越来越受欢迎 become more and more popular
    给……带来便利 bring convenience to
    面临挑战 face challenges
    采取措施 take measures/steps to
    引起广泛关注 attract/draw wide attention
    与……有关 be related/connected/associated with
    对……产生影响 have an impact/effect/influence on

    情感与观点¶

    中文 英文
    众所周知 it is well known that / as is known to all
    毫无疑问 there is no doubt that
    据我所知 as far as I know
    就我个人而言 as for me / personally speaking
    一方面……另一方面 on the one hand ... on the other hand
    更重要的是 what's more / more importantly
    总的来说 generally speaking / on the whole
    值得注意 it is worth noting that
    很难说 it is hard to say
    对……表示感谢 express one's gratitude/thanks to

    面④：翻译高频易错点¶

    错误类型 错误示例 正确写法
    时态错误 He go to school yesterday. He went to school yesterday.
    主谓一致 The number of students are increasing. The number of students is increasing.
    冠词遗漏 He is honest man. He is an honest man.
    中式英语 My body is very healthy. I am very healthy.
    介词搭配 succeed to do succeed in doing
    被动语态遗漏 The problem should solve. The problem should be solved.
    不可数名词加复数 many informations much information
    定语从句关系词 the city where I visited the city which/that I visited
    虚拟语气 If I was you, I would... If I were you, I would...
    固定搭配 look forward to do look forward to doing

    面⑤：翻译实战演练¶

    近年上海卷翻译真题风格¶

    年份 考点 示例
    2024 定语从句 + 非谓语 这部由年轻导演执导的电影获得了国际奖项。
    2023 状语从句 + 强调句 正是通过不断的努力，他才实现了自己的梦想。
    2022 倒装句 + 短语 我们只有团结一致，才能战胜这些困难。
    2021 非谓语 + 定语从句 正在图书馆里读书的那个女孩是我的同学。

    参考答案示例¶

    这部由年轻导演执导的电影获得了国际奖项。

    The movie, directed by a young director, has won an international award. (过去分词作定语)

    正是通过不断的努力，他才实现了自己的梦想。

    It was through constant effort that he realized his dream. (强调句型)

    我们只有团结一致，才能战胜这些困难。

    Only by working together can we overcome these difficulties. (only 倒装)

    每天5分钟翻译练习¶

    每天做3-5句翻译，重点：
Day 1-2: 定语从句 → 写5句
Day 3-4: 状语从句 → 写5句
Day 5-6: 非谓语动词 → 写5句
Day 7-8: 倒装句/强调句 → 写5句
Day 9-10: 综合练习 → 写5句（模拟真题）
