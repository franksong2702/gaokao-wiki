## 面①：核心动词 + 介词搭配（翻译/写作必用）

### 动词 + to

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| contribute **to** | 有助于；促成 | Hard work contributes **to** success. |
| lead **to** | 导致 | Smoking leads **to** many diseases. |
| refer **to** | 指的是；查阅 | The word "it" refers **to** the book. |
| devote **to** | 致力于 | She devoted her life **to** education. |
| object **to** | 反对 | Many people object **to** the plan. |
| adapt **to** | 适应 | You need to adapt **to** the new environment. |
| apply **to** | 适用于；申请 | The rule applies **to** everyone. |
| belong **to** | 属于 | This book belongs **to** me. |

### 动词 + for

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| account **for** | 占；解释 | Girls account **for** 60% of the students. |
| allow **for** | 考虑到 | We should allow **for** bad weather. |
| apologize **for** | 为……道歉 | He apologized **for** being late. |
| blame **for** | 归咎于 | Don't blame him **for** the mistake. |
| prepare **for** | 为……做准备 | Students are preparing **for** the exam. |
| stand **for** | 代表 | What does "AI" stand **for**? |
| qualify **for** | 有资格 | He qualifies **for** the scholarship. |

### 动词 + on/upon

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| depend **on** | 取决于；依靠 | Success depends **on** hard work. |
| focus **on** | 集中于 | Please focus **on** the main point. |
| rely **on** | 依赖 | We can rely **on** him. |
| insist **on** | 坚持 | He insisted **on** paying the bill. |
| comment **on** | 评论 | She commented **on** the new policy. |
| reflect **on** | 反思；反映 | This reflects **on** our education system. |
| concentrate **on** | 专注于 | Concentrate **on** your study. |

### 动词 + in

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| result **in** | 导致 | The accident resulted **in** three injuries. |
| participate **in** | 参加 | Students participate **in** various activities. |
| engage **in** | 从事 | He engaged **in** scientific research. |
| invest **in** | 投资于 | The company invested **in** new technology. |
| specialize **in** | 专攻 | She specializes **in** English literature. |
| consist **of** | 由……组成 | The team consists **of** five members. |
| believe **in** | 相信 | I believe **in** your ability. |

## 面③：易混词辨析（高频丢分点）

### 组1：rise / raise / arise

| 词 | 类型 | 含义 | 例句 |
|----|------|------|------|
| **rise** | 不及物 | 上升（自己升） | The sun rises in the east. |
| **raise** | 及物 | 举起；提高（使某物升） | Please raise your hand. |
| **arise** | 不及物 | 出现；产生（问题等） | A new problem has arisen. |

### 组2：affect / effect

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 例句 |
|----|------|------|------|
| **affect** | 动词 | 影响 | The weather affects our mood. |
| **effect** | 名词 | 效果；影响 | The drug has a good effect. |
| **effect** | 动词 | 实现；产生 | effect change（实现变革） |

### 组3：economic / economical / economy

| 词 | 词性 | 含义 | 例句 |
|----|------|------|------|
| **economic** | 形容词 | 经济的（宏观） | economic growth（经济增长） |
| **economical** | 形容词 | 节约的 | an economical car（省油的车） |
| **economy** | 名词 | 经济 | The economy is growing. |

### 组4：historic / historical

| 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|----|------|------|
| **historic** | 有历史意义的 | a historic moment（历史性时刻） |
| **historical** | 历史的（客观存在） | historical facts（历史事实） |

### 组5：adapt / adopt

| 词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|----|------|------|
| **adapt** | 适应；改编 | adapt to the new school（适应新学校） |
| **adopt** | 采用；收养 | adopt a new method（采用新方法） |

### 组6：assure / ensure / insure

| 词 | 含义 | 搭配 |
|----|------|------|
| **assure** | 向……保证 | assure sb. **of** sth. |
| **ensure** | 确保 | ensure **that** ... |
| **insure** | 给……投保 | insure one's life |

## 面⑤：写作必备连接词（概要+引导写作）

### 逻辑关系词

| 关系 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| **递进** | moreover / furthermore / in addition / besides / what's more |
| **转折** | however / nevertheless / on the contrary / while / whereas |
| **因果** | therefore / consequently / as a result / thus / hence |
| **举例** | for example / for instance / such as / namely / take ... as an example |
| **对比** | on the one hand ... on the other hand / in contrast / by comparison |
| **总结** | in conclusion / in summary / to sum up / all in all / generally speaking |
| **条件** | provided that / as long as / on condition that |
| **让步** | even though / despite / in spite of / regardless of |

### 概要写作必备句型

| 功能 | 句型 |
|------|------|
| 开头 | The passage mainly discusses/talks about ... |
| 作者观点 | The author argues/claims/suggests that ... |
| 列举 | First, ... Besides/In addition, ... Finally, ... |
| 转折 | However/Nevertheless, ... |
| 结论 | In short/In conclusion, ... |

### 引导写作（作文）开头句型

| 类型 | 句型 |
|------|------|
| 引出话题 | **Nowadays**, it is commonly believed that ... |
| 现象描述 | In recent years, there has been a growing trend/concern that ... |
| 引出争议 | When it comes to ..., opinions vary from person to person. |
| 个人观点 | As far as I am concerned, ... / From my perspective, ... |

### 引导写作结尾句型

| 类型 | 句型 |
|------|------|
| 总结观点 | Taking all these factors into consideration, we may safely conclude that ... |
| 呼吁 | It is high time that we took action to ... |
| 展望 | Only in this way can we ... |
